## The best salesperson is rarely the best manager yet

Most sales managers were promoted because they sold well. Leading other salespeople is a different craft, and the pressure of the number leaves little time to learn it. Coaching gives sales managers a confidential place to work on exactly that.

## Built around your team and your targets

Sessions use your real pipeline, your real people and your real quarter. We combine this coaching with [sales management training](/sales-management-training) for whole management teams where that is more appropriate.
