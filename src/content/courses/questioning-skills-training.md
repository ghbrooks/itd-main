## The skill behind every other skill

Coaching, selling, managing, advising and negotiating all depend on asking the right question at the right moment and then listening to the answer. Most of us run on habit. This workshop makes the skill conscious.

## Practical throughout

Participants bring the conversations where questioning matters most for them, and practise in small groups with feedback from the trainer.
