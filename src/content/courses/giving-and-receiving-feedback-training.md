## Feedback is a skill, not a personality trait

Some people are blunt, some avoid the subject entirely. Neither is feedback. This workshop gives everyone the same practical structure so that feedback is specific, timely and about behaviour that can change.

## Practice, not theory

Participants prepare a real piece of feedback they have been putting off, and practise delivering it. We also practise receiving feedback well, which is the half of the skill most training ignores.
