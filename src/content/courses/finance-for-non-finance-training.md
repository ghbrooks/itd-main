## Relevant and, genuinely, fun

Managers with budget responsibility need to understand the impact of their budgeting and how it fits into the wider business. The ITD approach to finance training is to make it highly relevant, by designing the training around the financial issues in your business, and fun, by making the mechanics engaging and lively.

## Before the workshop

- A meeting to discuss the participants’ needs and those of the organisation
- A draft workshop outline tailored to your needs
- Participants complete a short questionnaire on their challenges with finance

## After the workshop

Participants leave with individual actions. We follow up by email on personal action plans, and can add a follow-up call or one-to-one coaching where useful.
