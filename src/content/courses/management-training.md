## The Role of the Manager

We explore what it means to be an effective manager in today's workplace, covering the transition from individual contributor to manager and the key responsibilities that come with it.

## Core Management Skills

- Foundations of managing people
- Handling difficult situations and conversations
- Coaching and developing team members
- Managing performance effectively
- Delegating with confidence
- Giving and receiving feedback

## Participant Challenges

We build the training around the real challenges your managers face. Before the session, we often gather input from participants so the content is directly relevant to their work.
