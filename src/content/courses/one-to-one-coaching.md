## When group training is not the answer

Some development is too specific, too senior or too personal for a group workshop. One-to-one coaching gives you the trainer’s full attention on your situation, whether that is a pitch next month or a working pattern you have wanted to change for years.

## How it works

We start with a conversation about what you want to achieve and how we will both know it has worked. Sessions can be virtual or in person, and we set actions between them so the coaching keeps moving. See also [executive coaching](/executive-coaching) for senior leaders.
