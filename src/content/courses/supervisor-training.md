## Promoted for the wrong reasons

Most supervisors are promoted because they were good at the job. Supervising people is a different job, and very few are given any training for it. This workshop closes that gap with practical skills they can use the next day.

## Workshop style

Participants work on their own live situations: the team member who is always late, the task that keeps coming back half done, the colleague who used to be a peer and now reports to them. We give them a structure and they practise using it.
