## Why mentoring programmes stall

Most mentoring programmes start with enthusiasm and fade within a few months. The usual reasons are practical: nobody agreed what the relationship was for, meetings drift into a chat, and the mentee waits to be told what to do. This workshop fixes the practical problems.

## How we run it

We prefer to train mentors and mentees together so both sides leave with the same model and the same language. Participants practise a real mentoring conversation during the session, using their own situations rather than a role play script.

## Tailored to your programme

We adapt the content to your mentoring scheme, whether that is a structured firm-wide programme or a looser arrangement between senior and junior colleagues.
