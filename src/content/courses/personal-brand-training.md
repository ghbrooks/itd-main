## Reputation is built whether you plan it or not

Everyone has a personal brand. The question is whether it is the one you would choose. This workshop helps professionals take a deliberate view of their reputation and build it on purpose.

## Honest and practical

We use structured reflection and, where possible, feedback gathered before the session, so participants work from how they are actually perceived rather than how they hope they are. We also run a version for [lawyers](/personal-brand-training-for-lawyers).
