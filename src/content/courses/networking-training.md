## Most people hate it, which is the opportunity

If you can be the person at the event who is genuinely interested in others and follows up afterwards, you are already ahead of most of the room. This workshop gives reluctant networkers a method and a chance to practise it.

## Practice in the session

We rehearse real networking situations, including the walk into a room, the stuck conversation and the follow-up email. See also our [networking training for lawyers](/networking-training-for-lawyers).
