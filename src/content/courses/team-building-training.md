## Team building with a point

A good team session should change how people work on Monday, not just how they feel on Friday. We build these sessions around the real work of the team and use the [ITD Work Styles Model](/work-styles-model) so that everyone can see why colleagues approach things differently.

## Flexible format

We run team sessions as a half day, a full day, or as part of an offsite. Virtual team sessions work well too, using breakout rooms and shared whiteboards.
