## Knowing it is not the same as teaching it

The best subject experts are often the least effective trainers, because the material is obvious to them. This workshop gives them a method for designing training from the learner’s point of view and the delivery skills to make it stick.

## Everyone delivers

Each participant designs and delivers a short session during the workshop and gets structured feedback. For virtual delivery skills specifically, see [Train the Virtual Trainer](/train-the-virtual-trainer).
