## The delegation trap

"It's quicker to do it myself" is true once. By the tenth time, it has cost you a week and taught the other person nothing. This workshop looks at why capable people under-delegate and gives a practical structure for doing it well.

## What participants practise

Each participant brings a real task they need to delegate. They work through briefing it, deciding the level of autonomy and planning the checkpoints, then rehearse the briefing conversation with a colleague.
