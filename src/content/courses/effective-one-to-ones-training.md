## The meeting everyone has and nobody designed

Most managers inherited their one-to-one format from whoever managed them. The result is usually a workload update that could have been an email. This workshop redesigns the meeting around what it is actually for.

## What we cover

We work through a simple structure, a set of questions managers can keep in their back pocket, and the habits that keep the meeting useful over months rather than weeks. Managers practise with their own team situations.
