## You are closer to the client than the sales team

The people who hear about a client’s next problem first are rarely in sales. This workshop helps them recognise the opportunity, ask a few more questions and know what to do next, in a way that feels like good service rather than selling.

## Tailored to your business

We build the exercises around your services and your clients, so participants practise conversations they will actually have. We have delivered this training in the UK, Denmark, the Netherlands and Sweden.
