## Not another time management course

Time management courses tend to assume you control your diary. Most people do not. This workshop starts from the workload as it really is, with the requests, interruptions and shifting priorities, and gives practical ways to manage it.

## Built around your week

Participants map their own week and work on the specific pressure points. Where a team is trained together, we also agree team habits about requests, response times and meetings.
