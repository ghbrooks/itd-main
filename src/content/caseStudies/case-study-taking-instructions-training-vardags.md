## Challenge

Vardags needed their lawyers to improve their skills in taking client instructions, ensuring accuracy and building client confidence.

## Approach

ITD delivered taking instructions training covering questioning techniques, active listening, note-taking and managing sensitive conversations.

## Outcome

Lawyers reported improved client interactions and more accurate initial instructions, leading to fewer follow-up queries.
