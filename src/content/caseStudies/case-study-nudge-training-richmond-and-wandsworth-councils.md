## Challenge

Richmond and Wandsworth Councils wanted to apply nudge and behavioural science to improve resident engagement and service uptake.

## Approach

ITD delivered nudge training for council teams, demonstrating how small changes in communication and service design can significantly improve outcomes.

## Outcome

The councils successfully applied nudge techniques to several resident communications, seeing improved response rates.
