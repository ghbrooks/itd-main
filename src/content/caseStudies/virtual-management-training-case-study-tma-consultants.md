## Challenge

TMA Consultants had managers spread across multiple locations who needed consistent management training delivered virtually.

## Approach

ITD designed and delivered an engaging virtual management training programme that maintained high interaction levels despite the online format.

## Outcome

All participants rated the training as highly effective, with managers implementing new techniques within the first week.
