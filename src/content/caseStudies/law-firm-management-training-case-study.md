## Challenge

The firm needed to develop consistent management practices across departments to improve retention and staff development.

## Approach

ITD delivered a comprehensive management training programme tailored to the law firm context, covering performance management, delegation and team leadership.

## Outcome

The firm saw improved staff satisfaction scores and reduced associate turnover in the year following the programme.
