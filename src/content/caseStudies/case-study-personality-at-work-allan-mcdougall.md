## Challenge

Allan McDougall wanted to improve team cohesion by helping staff understand different personality types and working styles.

## Approach

ITD delivered a Personality at Work workshop helping participants recognise and value different approaches to work and communication.

## Outcome

The team reported better understanding of each other's strengths and preferred ways of working.
