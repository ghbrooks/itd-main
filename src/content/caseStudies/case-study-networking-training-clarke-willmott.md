## Challenge

Clarke Willmott wanted to help their lawyers develop networking skills to support business development and client relationship building.

## Approach

ITD delivered networking training covering preparation, conversation skills, follow-up strategies and building authentic professional relationships.

## Outcome

Lawyers reported increased confidence in networking situations and several generated new business leads directly following the training.
