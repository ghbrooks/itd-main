## Challenge

The Trust needed to improve the quality of visitor interactions and telephone handling across multiple school reception teams.

## Approach

ITD delivered visitor and contact care training covering first impressions, telephone techniques, handling difficult situations and maintaining professionalism.

## Outcome

Reception teams reported increased confidence and visitor satisfaction improved measurably across the Trust's schools.
