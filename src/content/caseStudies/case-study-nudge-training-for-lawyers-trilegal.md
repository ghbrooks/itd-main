## Challenge

Trilegal wanted to introduce nudge techniques to their lawyers to improve business development and client relationship management.

## Approach

ITD delivered nudge training specifically designed for the legal sector, covering behavioural science principles and their application in law firm business development.

## Outcome

Lawyers began applying nudge techniques to pitches and client meetings, reporting improved engagement and conversion rates.
