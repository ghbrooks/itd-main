## Challenge

Clarke Willmott wanted to help less confident team members develop their communication skills and professional presence.

## Approach

ITD delivered a supportive communication confidence programme covering assertiveness, speaking up in meetings and handling challenging conversations.

## Outcome

Participants reported significantly increased confidence in both internal and client-facing communication situations.
