## Challenge

Marbocote's managers needed to improve their communication skills to handle team conversations, feedback and performance discussions more effectively.

## Approach

ITD delivered management communication skills training covering assertive communication, giving feedback, conducting performance conversations and motivating teams.

## Outcome

Managers reported greater confidence in handling difficult conversations and team members noticed a positive shift in management communication style.
