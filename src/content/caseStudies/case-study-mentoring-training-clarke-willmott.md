## Challenge

Clarke Willmott wanted to improve the effectiveness of their mentoring programme through formal training for mentors.

## Approach

ITD delivered mentoring training covering mentoring techniques, structuring mentoring relationships and supporting mentee development.

## Outcome

The mentoring programme became significantly more effective, with both mentors and mentees reporting greater value from the relationships.
