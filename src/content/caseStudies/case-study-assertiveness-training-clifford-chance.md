challenge: 'A cohort of senior associates were technically excellent but visibly losing ground in difficult negotiations — particularly with longstanding partners and tough opposing counsel. Internal feedback flagged it as the single biggest blocker to partnership track. The firm wanted a programme that respected the seniority of the room and got to the point fast.'
approach: 'We ran a tailored two-day version of our assertiveness course, built specifically around the situations the cohort was facing — fee conversations, instructions from senior partners, and adversarial calls with opposing counsel. Pre-course interviews shaped the agenda; in the room we worked in trios, with each participant working three live, named situations of their own.'
outcome: 'Twelve months later, six of the eight cohort members had been promoted or were in the partnership pipeline. More immediately, internal 360 scores on “holds ground in negotiation” rose by an average of 1.4 points (out of 5) within six months. The firm subsequently rolled the course out to two further cohorts.'
