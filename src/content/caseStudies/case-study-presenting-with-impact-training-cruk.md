challenge: 'Senior research-translation specialists at CRUK had to present complex scientific findings to mixed-discipline audiences — funders, journalists, and partner organisations. The work was world-class; the delivery was burying it.'
approach: 'A two-day filmed presentation programme, structured around real upcoming presentations. Every participant brought a deck they would actually deliver in the next quarter. Day one rebuilt the structure; day two filmed and rebuilt the delivery, with one-to-one feedback after each pass.'
outcome: 'In a follow-up survey four months later, every participant reported applying the techniques in at least three real presentations. Several reported direct funding wins they attributed to the changes in how they opened and structured their pitches.'
