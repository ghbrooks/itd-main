Senior lawyers struggled to delegate effectively during complex transactions, leading to bottlenecks and junior lawyer frustration.

## Approach

ITD delivered targeted delegation skills training focused on the unique challenges of legal transactions, including task allocation, briefing and follow-up.

## Outcome

Partners reported smoother transaction workflows and associates felt more involved and better developed.
