## Challenge

Norgine wanted their managers to develop coaching skills to support the ongoing development of their teams.

## Approach

ITD delivered coaching skills training covering the GROW model, active listening, powerful questioning and building coaching relationships.

## Outcome

Managers adopted a more coaching-oriented management style, with improved team development and engagement.
