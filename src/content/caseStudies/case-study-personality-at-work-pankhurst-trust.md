## Challenge

Pankhurst Trust wanted to improve team dynamics by helping staff understand different personality types and communication preferences.

## Approach

ITD delivered a Personality at Work session helping participants understand their own and colleagues' working styles and communication preferences.

## Outcome

The team reported improved understanding of each other and more effective collaboration across different working styles.
