## Challenge

Newly promoted managers needed practical skills to handle day-to-day people management challenges effectively.

## Approach

ITD created a hands-on people management programme covering performance conversations, delegation, motivation and handling difficult situations.

## Outcome

The new managers reported feeling much more confident in their roles, with a measurable improvement in team engagement scores.
