## What is different about Nudge training with ITD?

We're often asked what's different about Nudge Training from ITD? So here's some thoughts:

### Simple to understand

We make Nudge very simple to understand. Nudge Theory was developed by some serious brainy people. It involves a lot of ideas and research interwoven. To explain it could take a lot of complexity. But at ITD we make it really simple, by breaking it down into learning nuggets.

### Easy to apply

At ITD we make these ideas easy to apply. By breaking Nudge down into its composite parts, it means that we can make it that much easier to apply.

### Explain, Illustrate, Apply

Building on that, we help you to apply each Nudge idea in the training. So we introduce a Nudge idea, explain it, illustrate it, then help you to apply it to your organisation and challenges.

### Practical

Our Nudge Training is practical. Yes we cover the theory, but then we take it further by applying it to your scenarios. So it's a workshop not just a course. Interaction is throughout the session.

### Applied to your challenges

By the end of the session you leave with some really practical ways to apply Nudge to your challenges.

### Experience

We use our experience of working with clients and their challenges, plus our knowledge of Nudge, to make it a truly inspirational session.
