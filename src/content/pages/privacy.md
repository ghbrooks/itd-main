ITD Limited ("ITD", "we", "us") is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 4534876, PO Box 282, Chipping Norton, OX7 9EE. You can contact us at [info@itd.com](mailto:info@itd.com).

## What we collect

- **Enquiries.** When you contact us by form, email or phone we keep your name, organisation, contact details and the content of your message so that we can respond and, if you become a client, deliver and administer the training.
- **Reviews.** If you submit a review, we publish the review text and the name you give us. We do not publish email addresses.
- **Questionnaires.** The Work Styles Model questionnaire on this site is scored in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to share them.
- **Website usage.** Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) for security and performance. We do not run advertising trackers.

## How we use it

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, to deliver and administer training and coaching, to keep business records we are legally required to keep, and to send occasional updates about our training where you have asked for them. Our lawful bases are performance of a contract, our legitimate interests in running the business, and consent where we rely on it.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with suppliers who help us run the business (for example, hosting, email and accounting providers) under contracts that protect it, and where the law requires.

## Retention

We keep enquiry and client records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, typically no longer than seven years after our last contact.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict our use of it, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. Email [info@itd.com](mailto:info@itd.com). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Cookies

This website uses no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. The site may store a small amount of data in your browser to remember preferences such as a filter you have chosen; this stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. The date above shows when it was last revised.
