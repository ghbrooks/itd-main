Martin is a co-founder and Managing Director of ITD. He is an experienced consultant with a broad range of projects to draw on, and has worked with many diverse organisations in many countries and cultures.

He trains on many subjects under the banner of soft skills or business skills, from management training to Nudge. He has worked with managers in Manchester, scientists in San Francisco, lawyers in Paris, sales people in Milwaukee, paralegals in Abu Dhabi, IT engineers in Brussels, HR specialists in Athens, board directors in Amsterdam and secretaries in London.

Martin is always on the look out for new ideas, or new ways of explaining existing ideas. He is the driving force behind ITD and the first contact with most new clients.

## Subjects Martin trains and speaks on

- Communication skills, including professional business communication and written communication
- Presentation skills, in person and in the virtual world
- Leadership and management, including leading remote teams
- Personal efficiency and time management
- Personality at work, using the [ITD Work Styles Model](/work-styles-model)
- Nudge: applying behavioural science to everyday decisions

## Keynote speaking

Martin's keynote sessions are inspired by ITD's training: practical, relevant and tailored to the audience. Popular talks include Presenting in a Virtual World, Personality at Work, Nudge, Leading in a Virtual World and Personal Efficiency. See [keynote speaker](/keynote-speaker) for more.
