## Training We Deliver

- ITD delivers programmes in client relationship management, business development and networking skills, presentation and advocacy, leadership development for partners and senior associates, managing and developing junior lawyers, and cross-selling and pitching. We also offer coaching for lawyers stepping into management and leadership roles for the first time.
- Programmes can be delivered as firm-wide initiatives, practice group sessions, or individual coaching engagements.

## Sector-Specific Challenges

- Lawyers are trained to analyse and advise, but the skills that make a successful legal career extend far beyond technical ability. Partners need to win and retain work, manage client expectations, and lead teams. Associates must develop their profile, manage upwards, and build the confidence to present to senior audiences. Across the firm, people need the ability to influence, negotiate, and communicate complex advice in plain language.
- ITD understands the unique culture and pressures of legal practice and designs training that addresses these challenges head-on.

## Why ITD for Law Firms

ITD has worked with law firms across the UK and internationally, earning a reputation for training that is commercially focused, intellectually stimulating, and highly practical. Our facilitators understand the professional standards and time pressures of legal practice, and they deliver sessions that lawyers value and engage with. Firms return to ITD because our training produces measurable improvements in client satisfaction, team leadership, and business development outcomes.
