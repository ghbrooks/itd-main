## Training We Deliver

- ITD delivers programmes in leadership development for tech leads and engineering managers, presentation skills for product demos and stakeholder updates, influencing and negotiation for product and project managers, cross-functional collaboration, and client-facing communication for professional services teams.
- We also offer executive coaching for CTOs and senior technology leaders who want to strengthen their presence and strategic communication skills.

## Sector-Specific Challenges

- Technology professionals are often promoted into leadership roles on the strength of their technical ability, only to find that managing people and communicating across the business require an entirely different skill set. Engineers need to explain complex architectures to non-technical stakeholders. Product managers must influence without direct authority. Remote and hybrid teams need to collaborate effectively across time zones and cultures.
- ITD addresses these challenges with practical, evidence-based training that resonates with analytical minds.

## Why ITD for Technology

ITD understands the technology sector and the mindset of its people. Our facilitators are comfortable in environments where rigour and evidence matter, and they deliver training that is structured, practical, and free of jargon. We have worked with technology teams across software development, SaaS, fintech, cybersecurity, and telecommunications, and we consistently receive outstanding feedback from delegates who value our direct, no-nonsense approach.
