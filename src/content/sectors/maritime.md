## Training We Deliver

- Our maritime programmes include leadership development for shore-based and sea-going managers, safety communication and just culture training, cross-cultural communication for multinational crews, presentation skills for commercial and technical teams, and stakeholder engagement for port and harbour authorities.
- We deliver training both onshore and at maritime facilities, and our virtual programmes support globally dispersed teams.

## Typical Challenges and Needs

- Maritime organisations manage operations that span the globe, often with culturally diverse crews and shore-based teams working across multiple time zones. Communication breakdowns can have serious safety consequences, and the hierarchical traditions of the industry sometimes inhibit the open dialogue that modern safety culture demands.
- ITD helps maritime organisations develop leaders who communicate clearly, build trust across cultural boundaries, and foster environments where people feel confident to speak up.

## Why ITD for Maritime

ITD has built a strong reputation in the maritime sector through sustained partnerships with some of the industry's most respected organisations. Our facilitators understand the operational realities, regulatory frameworks, and cultural dynamics of maritime environments. We design programmes that are practical, relevant, and sensitive to the traditions of the industry while promoting the modern leadership and communication practices that drive safety and performance.
