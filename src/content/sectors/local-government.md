## Training We Deliver

- ITD provides local government clients with programmes in leadership development, management skills, presentation and public speaking, community engagement, influencing and negotiation, and handling difficult conversations. We also deliver training that supports organisational transformation, helping managers lead their teams through restructures and service redesigns.
- Our programmes can be delivered on-site at council offices, at external venues, or through virtual platforms to accommodate flexible working arrangements.

## Sector-Specific Challenges

- Local government professionals often work under significant resource constraints while facing growing demand for services. They must navigate complex political environments, balance the needs of multiple stakeholder groups, and maintain morale in teams that are frequently subject to restructuring.
- ITD designs training that acknowledges these realities and provides practical tools for managing upwards, engaging with elected members, and maintaining personal resilience.

## Why ITD for Local Government

ITD understands the public-sector context and the particular pressures that local government professionals face. Our facilitators bring empathy and pragmatism to every session, ensuring delegates feel understood and leave with strategies they can apply immediately. We have worked with councils across England, Scotland, and Wales, delivering programmes that create lasting improvements in leadership confidence and service quality.
