## Training We Deliver

- Our pharma and biotech programmes include scientific presentation skills, leadership development for R&D and commercial managers, medical affairs communication, cross-functional team effectiveness, influencing and stakeholder management, and executive presence coaching for senior leaders.
- We design programmes that respect the scientific rigour of the sector while helping delegates communicate with clarity, conviction, and appropriate simplicity.

## Sector-Specific Challenges

- Pharma and biotech professionals must navigate an environment of intense regulation, long development timelines, and high commercial stakes. Scientists need to translate complex data into clear narratives for diverse audiences. Medical affairs teams must communicate within strict compliance frameworks. Leaders managing matrix organisations need exceptional influencing skills to align teams that sit across functions, geographies, and sometimes partner organisations.
- ITD helps organisations develop these capabilities through training that is grounded in the realities of the life sciences industry.
