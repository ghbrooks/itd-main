## Training We Deliver

- Our general business portfolio covers the full spectrum of soft skills: presentation skills, management and leadership development, communication and influencing, time management, negotiation, and team building. We also design bespoke programmes that combine multiple skill areas into cohesive development journeys for specific teams or cohorts.
- Delivery formats include face-to-face workshops, virtual instructor-led sessions, and blended programmes that combine both with self-directed digital resources.

## Common Challenges We Address

- Many organisations find that technical expertise alone is not enough to drive performance. Managers promoted for their subject-matter knowledge often struggle with delegation, feedback, and motivating their teams. Cross-functional projects stall because stakeholders lack the influencing skills to align competing priorities.
- ITD helps organisations close these gaps by equipping people at every level with the interpersonal skills they need to perform at their best.

## Why ITD for General Business

ITD brings a consultative approach to every engagement. We take the time to understand your business context, culture, and objectives before designing a programme. Our facilitators are experienced business professionals who combine credibility with engaging delivery, ensuring delegates leave the training room with practical tools they can apply immediately.
