## Training We Deliver

- Our media sector programmes include leadership development for creative directors and team managers, client relationship and account management skills, presentation and pitching, influencing and negotiation, time management under deadline pressure, and team collaboration workshops. We also deliver coaching for senior leaders navigating the transformation of traditional media businesses.
- Programmes are designed to be engaging and fast-paced, reflecting the energy and culture of the media industry.

## Typical Challenges and Needs

- Media professionals often work in high-pressure, deadline-driven environments where creative tensions, client demands, and commercial pressures collide. Managers may be outstanding creatives who find the transition to people leadership challenging. Client-facing teams need the confidence to present bold ideas persuasively and manage feedback constructively. In an industry undergoing rapid digital transformation, leaders also need the skills to guide their teams through constant change.
- ITD helps media organisations develop people who can balance creative ambition with commercial pragmatism and lead with confidence in a fast-moving environment.

## Why ITD for Media

ITD has a strong track record with media organisations across broadcast, print, digital, and advertising. Our facilitators are engaging, direct, and creatively minded, which means they connect quickly with media professionals. We design programmes that respect the pace and culture of the industry while challenging delegates to develop new skills and perspectives. Media clients choose ITD because our training is practical, stimulating, and delivers visible improvements in team dynamics, client satisfaction, and leadership effectiveness.
