## Training We Deliver

- Our charity sector programmes include leadership development for senior managers and emerging leaders, presentation skills for fundraising pitches and trustee briefings, stakeholder engagement, managing volunteers, and influencing without authority. We also deliver resilience and wellbeing workshops tailored to the emotional demands of charity work.
- ITD offers flexible delivery options and competitive pricing structures that recognise the budgetary realities of the non-profit sector.

## Typical Challenges and Needs

- Charity leaders frequently step into management roles because of their expertise in programme delivery rather than their people management experience. They may need to lead teams of both paid staff and volunteers, navigate governance relationships with trustees, and present compelling cases for funding to corporate and institutional donors.
- ITD helps charities build the internal capability to manage these challenges effectively, creating stronger leadership pipelines and more confident communicators throughout the organisation.

## Why ITD for Charities

ITD is proud to work with charities of all sizes, from small community organisations to large national bodies. We bring the same quality of facilitation and programme design that we offer to our corporate clients, while adapting our approach to reflect the values, culture, and resource constraints of the charity sector. Our clients tell us that our training helps them retain talented staff, strengthen governance, and improve the way they tell their story to the world.
