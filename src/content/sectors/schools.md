## Training We Deliver

- ITD provides schools with training in leadership and management for heads, deputies, and middle leaders, presentation and public speaking skills, communication with parents and governors, handling difficult conversations, and team development for staff groups. We also offer coaching programmes for newly appointed headteachers and senior leaders.
- Programmes can be delivered during INSET days, twilight sessions, or across a series of shorter workshops to minimise disruption to teaching timetables.

## Sector-Specific Challenges

- School leaders manage large, diverse teams while navigating accountability frameworks, parental expectations, and budget pressures. Middle leaders are often excellent classroom practitioners who need support in transitioning to management roles. All staff benefit from being able to communicate with confidence in a range of contexts, from parent evenings to Ofsted inspections to staff meetings.
- ITD understands the school environment and designs training that acknowledges both the rewards and the pressures of working in education.

## Why ITD for Schools

ITD has delivered training in schools across the UK, from individual schools to large multi-academy trusts. Our facilitators bring warmth, credibility, and a deep respect for the teaching profession. We design programmes that use relevant scenarios and examples, ensuring delegates can see exactly how the skills apply to their roles. Schools return to ITD because our training makes a tangible difference to staff confidence, team cohesion, and leadership effectiveness.
