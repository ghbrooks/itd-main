## Training We Deliver

- Our university programmes include leadership development for heads of department and research group leaders, management skills for professional services managers, presentation skills for lectures and conferences, influencing and stakeholder management, and team effectiveness workshops for cross-departmental groups.
- We also deliver bespoke programmes for postgraduate researchers and early-career academics who are developing their professional skills alongside their research.

## Typical Challenges and Needs

- Universities operate with distinctive governance structures and a culture that values academic freedom and collegial decision-making. This means that leadership often requires exceptional influencing skills rather than positional authority. Professional services staff must collaborate across faculties and departments, often managing change in environments where consensus is hard to achieve.
- ITD designs training that respects the academic context while providing practical tools for leading, communicating, and collaborating more effectively.

## Why ITD for Universities

ITD has worked with a wide range of UK and international universities, delivering programmes that are valued for their intellectual rigour, practical relevance, and engaging facilitation. Our facilitators are comfortable working with highly analytical audiences and bring a style that combines challenge with support. Universities choose ITD because we understand the sector and deliver training that helps both individuals and institutions perform at their best.
